Key Summary/Results

We compared the changes in brain size within a dataset of 78 patients to see if a correlation in brain size changes can explain changes in Alzheimer's Disease progression. We found a direct correlation, particularly within the sizes of 2.5 lbs to 3 lbs, as people with a higher chance of getting Alzheimer's Disease when they are older. This correlated to about 66% of the entire population within the dataset.

Future Work & Considerations

Some points for future work include exploring other lifestyle factors regarding the changes in brain size, gaining work on a bigger dataset to compare more sizes and ranges of brain sizes, and hopefully, investigating longer ranges of age groups to see a more direct progressional change setup.
